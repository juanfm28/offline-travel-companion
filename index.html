Check before travelling offline

The app checks saved shell assets and reads back your imported files. This does not replace an airplane-mode test on your device.

No file check run in this session.

  1. Disconnect the hotspot and enable Airplane Mode. Turn Wi-Fi off too.
  2. Force-close this app and reopen it from the Home Screen.
  3. Open the itinerary, test its map and navigation, and read several PDFs.
  4. Restart the device while offline and repeat. Recheck after several days.
